# Welcome to my research website.

My name is Balaje Kalyanaraman and I am a PhD student at the
University of Newcastle, Australia. I did my M.Sc (Hons) Mathematics
and B.E (Hons) Mechanical Engineering from BITS Pilani - Goa Campus. My
interests lie in the field of numerical methods to solve partial
differential equations.

Feel free to look around. You can start with
my [Resume here](/about.html); short description of my
current [research interests](/research.html) here; my
blog [here](/blog-page.html). You can also check out my github
profile [@github.com/Balaje](https://github.com/Balaje) and my Google scholar
[@scholar/Balaje.](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=h_FPNVAAAAAJ&hl=en)
