As of writing this post, I am officially not affiliated to any university! It has been a long overdue (an understatement of the century) for a blog post. If you are keen-eyed, the last blog post was on December 2021, which is at this point almost 5 years ago! Safe to say that I did get some training to be a professor [📖 Insert Relevant PhD comic 📖](https://phdcomics.com/comics/archive.php?comicid=1933). What do I write? A nice blog post summarizing the last 5 years should be nice. But before that, something else needs to be addressed here in regards to my website. I took sometime to think hard about what I want to have on my website going forward. I initially thought about writing about my research (and math in general) regularly in a more accessible manner. However at the time, I did not realise how hard it actually is.

Let us take this example. I spoke about the Shape from Shading example in my [previous post](/2021/12/08/Shape-from-shading-revisited). While writing the blog post, I wanted to somehow summarize this huge topic within a single blog post -- which is definitely impossible. Even my 3-month long [GSoC Project](/gsoc/index) was divided into 5 blog posts! If I re-read it after these many years, I still think it is a good summary. But it is completely useless to someone who knows nothing about the kind of mathematics that goes behind the PDE formulation and the numerical technique. The post just effectively tells the reader that there is a problem called Shape from Shading -- and here are our results. And nothing else is really available in the blog post to take away! The existence of viscosity solutions and the construction of the numerical method is the most important aspect as a mathematician. And that is completely missing! So I decided that I might actually take the time to write more coherent articles related to my research rather than (trying to) condense them into a single blog post.

That being said, I have reached a point where content needs to be more focussed, I have decided to archive blog posts that I wrote during my undergraduate. So anything related to OpenCV and image processing will no longer be there. This doesn't mean that I wont come back to these projects ever. From today, you can see my posts starting from PhD, which will be phased out as I move on. Since I host this using Github Pages, you should technically be able to access the markdown files of those posts from the commit history (depending on whether I choose to keep my repository public/private). From here on, blogs will merely be a supplement to deeper research-related content in my platform. 

Until then, please have a look at my updated website. See you soon!
